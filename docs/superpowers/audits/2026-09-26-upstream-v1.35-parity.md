and the weekly tracker's "N commits behind" count only ever grow. This ledger is
the record of what has actually been carried across. Every row was checked
against the fork's code, not its history.

tracking issue diffed `main` alone, so none of v1.35 had appeared in it; the

### Where the link tiles differ from upstream
- **Placement.** The fork's home shows pinned apps only. Tiles are always
  pinned and sit in their own Your links deck, after the app decks, with no
  pin toggle. Upstream's dashboard has no decks, so it had nothing to decide.
- **Left out of the Supply Depot, the Apps page and the nightly update check.**
  Upstream lists tiles in the Supply Depot, where every action would act on a
  container that does not exist, and checks each one's empty image for updates.
- **Edit and Remove always visible**, not on hover, for touch screens.
- **One URL check** (`util/custom_url.ts`) for the server and the form, where
  upstream keeps a copy on each side. It also reports why it refused a URL, so a
  URL that is only too long is not called invalid.
- **Validators in `validators/link_tile.ts`**, so `validators/system.ts` stays
  loadable by the standalone harness. **Routes gated** with `localNetworkOnly`.
  **409 and 422** reach the user in the server's words.

## Device pass (everything above is typechecked and unit-checked, none of it rendered or run on a Mac)
1. A long answer runs past ~750 words; a forced cut shows the notice and Continue.
2. Leaving mid-answer: `ollama ps` goes idle on Ollama ≥ 0.33.3; on an older
   build the log names `nomad upgrade ollama` and the answer completes.
3. A knowledge-base answer shows Sources, which survive a reload; the
   `sources` migration ran.
4. Dark mode: chat links, Continue, and the Benchmark Builder Tag are legible.
5. Delete an indexed ZIM: its source is gone from Qdrant with no Sync. Install
   a newer edition: the old passages go. Rename `zim` and Sync: nothing purged.
6. Index a navigation-heavy ZIM (Medicine LibreTexts): it continues past batch one.
7. Content Explorer: Language picker lists languages; switching restarts the
   list; offline it hides and Retry brings it back.
8. `node ace eval:retrieval` in the container resolves sources.
9. Link tiles (the two `services` migrations ran): a tile added as
   `192.168.1.50:8080` opens `http://192.168.1.50:8080/` in a new tab and is
   still there a minute later. It shows in the grid and in Your links, and is
   absent from the Supply Depot and Apps. Edit, Remove and the confirm work by
   touch on a phone. A duplicate name, and a URL over 255 characters, each get
   their own message.

## Decisions for Chris
- **932ced39**: take upstream's `maxStalledCount: 0` for embeddings, or keep
  the fork's deliberate 3 and make point IDs deterministic so a re-run
  overwrites instead of duplicating.
- **Offline translation**: whether to build and publish an arm64
  `nomad-translate` image, given model size and licensing.
- **Release**: Unreleased now carries the 1,024-token fix for a regression
  0.2.765 shipped; worth cutting 0.2.766 after the device pass.
- **Tracking**: open a v1.35 port issue like caweis#48, or let this ledger serve.
- **Link tile placement** (decided here, easy to change): always pinned, in a
  deck of their own, with no pin toggle. Mixing tiles into the app decks, or
  letting them be unpinned, would first need somewhere else to find an
  unpinned tile, since the Supply Depot leaves them out.
